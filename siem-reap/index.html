Swimming Pools

Dorm wing pool:

Enjoy a stunning 360-degree view of Siem Reap! Our rooftop pool is perfect for relaxation after exploring the temples.

Choose between two poolside spaces: Rooftop 4th Floor and Ground Floor. Open daily from 8:00 AM to 9:45 PM.

Private wing pool:

For a quieter experience, our ground floor pool offers a peaceful escape with less sun exposure. Ideal for relaxation, also open from 8:00 AM to 9:45 PM.

24/7 Reception

Our reception is open 24/7 to make your stay as comfortable and stress-free as possible. Whether you’re checking in late, heading out early, or need assistance during your stay, our friendly team is always available to help. From answering your questions to providing local tips and support, we’re here for you around the clock.

Tour and travel desk

Our tour desk offers essential information and arrangements for tuk-tuk tours to local attractions. We also provide bus tickets to various destinations like Sihanoukville and Phnom Penh, with many companies offering pick-up services at our hostel.

Cafe and Bar

Relax at our hostel’s cafe and bar, where you can enjoy freshly brewed coffee, refreshing draft beer, and creative cocktails. Coffee starts at 1.25 USD, cocktails from 1.5 USD, and draft beer for just 1 USD. A perfect spot to unwind and socialize with fellow guests.

Restaurant

Our restaurant offers delicious meals and drinks at great prices, served in a spacious and cozy setting. Open from 6:30 am to 10:45 pm, with a special sunrise breakfast from 4:00 am to 4:30 am for early risers heading to Angkor Wat. Enjoy a comfortable and friendly dining experience.

Common Area

Our spacious ground floor common area features a beautiful garden terrace and a great restaurant. It’s the perfect spot to meet fellow travelers, enjoy meals, read, or unwind with various facilities catering to your needs.

Luandry service

Our laundry service is available at $1.50 per kg, with a turnaround time of 24 hours. Just bring your clothes to reception to get started!

Shared bathroom (for dorm rooms)

Every floor (our dorm rooms are on the Ground, 1st, 2nd and 3rd floor) has a lady bathroom and a man bathroom. Every bathroom has 3 shower rooms with electric shower system, 3 toilet rooms and 2 lavatories.

Body soap, shampoo and hair dryers are available for free. We also provide towel for free with 5 usd deposit.

Parking

Parking for bikes is available for guests staying at the hostel. Feel free to use it during your stay!

Dormitory Room

We provide comfortable sleeping space to everyone. Our bunk beds are like boxes that can make you feel having some privacy. You will find electric socket, reading lamp, and accessory case in your sleeping space. Although we are hostel, our mattresses, blankets, and pillows are as good as items in a hotel. All of our rooms are equipped with well-maintained air-conditioner and exhaust fan. ...
We placed shared bathrooms outside the dorm room so your sleep will not be disturbed by noise from bathrooms.
Room Locker : We provide large lockers measuring 40cm*50cm*80cm. We also provide padlocks for those who don't have their own. So, you will get 2 keys when you check in: one for the room door and another for the locker.

Private Room

Our private rooms have air conditioner, TV, electronic digital security box, electric kettle (with coffee and tea set), hair dryer, 2 bottles of water, free Wifi, towels and etc...
We have a private bathroom inside the room. We have partition wall and curtain between shower booth and toilet.

Experience in Siem Reap

Small Circuit

Every day

Explore the beautiful sunrise at Angkor Wat Temple with a shared or private tour. The tour includes the most popular temples: Angkor Wat, Bayon, and Ta Prohm.

Grand Circuit

Every day

Visit the beautiful sunset at Phnom Bakheng Temple, located on a hill. The tour includes five temples, known as the Grand Circuit: Preah Khan, Ta Som, Neak Pean, Pre Rup, and Phnom Bakheng.

Kulen Waterfall Tour

Tuesday, Friday

Visit Kulen Waterfall in Siem Reap for its stunning natural beauty, crystal-clear pools, and rich cultural significance. It’s a perfect spot to relax, swim, and connect with Cambodia's history, while enjoying the peaceful surroundings of Phnom Kulen National Park.

Cycling Tour

Monday, Thursday, Saturday

Cycling through the countryside allows you to enjoy the local landscape and fields. You can learn about the beliefs of the Khmer people and have dinner with a sunset view at the lotus flower farm.

Street Food Tour

Wednesday, Sunday

Immerse yourself in Cambodia's vibrabt sreet food culture on our 3-hours evening tour! Join our team for an epic cuilnary jouney through Siem Reap.

Bracelet Class

Wednesday, Sunday

Join us for a fun and creative bracelet-making class where you'll learn the basics of designing and crafting your own unique bracelets. Whether you’re a beginner or have some experience, this class is perfect for anyone looking to express their personal style.

Yoga Class

Monday, Friday

Yoga promotes overall well-being by reducing stress, enhancing flexibility, and fostering a deeper mind-body connection. It encourages mindfulness, builds strength, and supports emotional balance, making it a holistic practice for personal growth and community connection.

Social Night

Monday, Wednesday, Saturday

Join us for a fun and free Social Night at the hostel! Enjoy an exciting quiz game where you can test your knowledge and compete with fellow guests. The winner will receive a special prize!
Don’t miss this chance to relax, meet new friends, and have a great time. See you there!

Monk Blessing

Tuesday, Friday

Experience the sacred tradition of monk blessings, a deeply rooted Cambodian belief. Through chants, holy water, and prayers, monks offer peace, protection, and prosperity. This meaningful ceremony is believed to bring spiritual cleansing, good fortune, and positive energy into your life, following cherished Cambodian traditions.

Location

4 minutes walk to PUB STREET.

The new airport is located 45 kilometers from Siem Reap city center. ...

From airport :
You can reach the city by private taxi or shuttle bus. Private taxi costs 35 USD while shuttle bus costs 8 USD (15 USD for 2 ways).

The shuttle bus departure times are as follows. 9:30, 12:30, 15:00, 20:00. There are several bus stops but please get off at CDF Duty Free Store and it will take around 7 mins by tuktuk to the hostel (1.2km)

From bus station :
For bus or mini van: The bus drop-off location varies depending on the bus company. Most are approximately 1-4 km from the city center. Please take a tuktuk and it costs around 2-3 usd.
